# Bloated_Blog 
# DH-Skills-and-Methods W8 Lab Session

In this lab, you will think about minimising energy usage in DH website design, following minimal computing recommendations from the Digital Humanities Climate Coalition. Just as the University of Glasgow has a carbon management plan, so does our classroom, and you will actively contribute to reaching our greenhouse emission reduction target! 

This will involve using the online carbon calculator Ecograder as a benchmark, and  editing this bloated, heavy, sample website to reduce its emissions: minifying its design through green coding approaches.

ILOs - 

To understand how to make web resources more environmental friendly and less carbon intensive, using minimal computing and green code approaches

To become familiar with web-hosting environments and code repositories 

To relate environmental sustainability to broader digital sustainability concerns
